Privacy Policy — TNX1 Rig

Last updated: 2026-11-07

Summary

TNX1 Rig does not collect, store on servers or share any personal data. There is no account, no ads, no usage analytics and no tracking.

How the app works

The app talks directly to your pedal (over a USB cable) and to your MIDI controller (over Bluetooth). These communications happen only between your phone and your equipment; the app sends nothing to the internet.

Data kept on the device

The app keeps only your preferences on the phone itself (language, whether the MIDI controller should connect automatically, and whether Pro is unlocked). The technical message log stays on the device and leaves it only if you choose to share it.

Permissions

USB: talk to the pedal. Bluetooth: find and connect the MIDI controller (the scan is not used to learn your location). Notifications: show that the bridge to the pedal is active. Foreground service (connected device): keep the connections alive with the screen off. Microphone: declared only so Android remembers the USB authorization of the pedal, which is also an audio interface; the app does not record or process audio.

Pro purchase

The TNX1 Rig Pro purchase is made and processed by Google Play. The app does not receive your payment details; it only asks Google Play whether the purchase exists and keeps the result (unlocked or not) on the device.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not collect data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this same address with an updated date.
